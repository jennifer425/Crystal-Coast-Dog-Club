About / Our Story

This started with a Great Dane named Maddox and a lot of vacations we didn't want to end.

Photo: maddox-beach.jpg
Maddox the Great Dane and Jennifer on the beach at Emerald Isle
How it started

Before Carteret County was home, it was where Jennifer and her family vacationed. Every summer, Emerald Isle, Maddox the Great Dane in tow. What she noticed wasn't just the beach — it was how naturally dog-friendly everything felt. Dogs weren't an afterthought here. They were just part of life.

Once she moved here full time, she noticed something else: a lot of people wanted to support local rescue but didn't know where to start. So she built the bridge.

Photo: emerald-isle-pier.png
Emerald Isle beach town pier at sunset
The turning point

Visibility matters. The more people see these dogs, the more they get adopted.

That's the belief Crystal Coast Dog Club grew out of. Not another heavy nonprofit. Not a traditional rescue org. A community where doing good feels joyful, where you find your people, and where dogs are the reason it all works.

Dogs bring down walls. They start conversations between people who'd otherwise never meet. We believe rescue support doesn't have to come from an exhausting place — joy and impact can live in the same room.
