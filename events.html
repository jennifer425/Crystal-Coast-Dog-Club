Signature events

A few times a year, we go bigger

Art to the Rescue Evening

Our once-a-year celebration. Art, good food, live music, and dogs everywhere you look. Casual glam, big heart, real impact for local rescue.

Vendor Market Day

Twice a year, spring and fall. Local businesses, dog-friendly vendors, a whole day built around browsing with your best friend at your side.

The Wildcard

Every year we try something different. A Mutt Strut, a putt-putt tournament, whatever sounds fun that season. If we love it, it might just stick around.
